## 6. Honest Statement of Predictive Power

The previous committed results (R² ≈ 0.94) described only in-sample split performance on a 25-sample dataset. Validation with **leave-one-temperature-out cross-validation** — the appropriate test of generalization to a new processing temperature — gives:

- Linear Regression: MAE 5.23, R² 0.32
- Random Forest: MAE 8.10, R² −0.26 (tree ensembles do not extrapolate to unseen temperatures)

The phase-fraction models are therefore **useful for interpolation within the 700–1100 °C window, but not yet predictive across unseen processing temperatures**. This limitation is now stated explicitly in `README.md` and reflected in `data/processed/model_results.csv`; it defines the next research step (more temperatures, physics-informed features) rather than being hidden.

The mechanistic component — Korsmeyer–Peppas transport modeling — is well validated (R² ≥ 0.999 on the committed time series) and is the strongest quantitative result in the repository.
