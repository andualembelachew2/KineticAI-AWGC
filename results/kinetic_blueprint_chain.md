# Kinetic Blueprint Chain

## Processing

Thermal processing temperature was varied from 700°C to 1100°C.

↓

## Phase Architecture

Increasing temperature reduced amorphous content and increased crystalline phase fractions.

↓

## Structural Consolidation

Density increased from 2.02 g/cm³ to 2.80 g/cm³.

↓

## Transport Behavior

700°C:
- n = 0.25
- Quasi-Fickian transport

1100°C:
- n = 0.58
- Anomalous transport

↓

## Microenvironment Evolution

700°C:
- Peak pH = 8.55

1100°C:
- Peak pH = 7.82

↓

## Scientific Outcome

Thermal phase partitioning modifies transport behavior and microenvironment evolution.

This sequence represents the experimental foundation of the Kinetic Blueprint Framework.
